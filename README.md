#### Project Overview: 
###### This "Strategic Retail & Consumer Insights" dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of $233K in revenue across multiple product categories and seasons. The goal of this project was to transform raw retail data into an interactive decision-making tool for stakeholders.

#### Key Findings:

###### Revenue Drivers: Clothing is the dominant category, contributing $104K (nearly 45%) of total sales, followed by Accessories.

###### Seasonal Peaks: Fall is the highest-performing season ($60K), while Summer shows a slight dip ($56K), suggesting a need for mid-year promotional strategies.

###### Customer Behavior: Payment methods are highly balanced, but Credit Cards lead at 17.3%, indicating a customer base that favors traditional financing over digital wallets like Venmo.

###### Promotion Efficacy: Interestingly, average ratings remain stable at 3.7–3.8 regardless of discount status, proving that while discounts drive volume, they do not negatively impact the perceived value of the brand.
